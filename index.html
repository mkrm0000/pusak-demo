Who we are

A unified platform for Kamalganj's public university students.

PUSAK brings together current and former public university and medical students from Kamalganj. The organization works as a bridge between students, alumni, educators, community leaders, and local institutions.

Its mission is to promote education, leadership, awareness, social responsibility, and long-term development for Kamalganj.

Programmes built around learning, leadership, and service.

Mission

Empower students to serve society with knowledge and integrity.

PUSAK encourages students to become thoughtful, skilled, and socially responsible leaders.

Vision

A connected student network contributing to Kamalganj's development.

We imagine a future where every student can access guidance, opportunity, and a supportive community.
